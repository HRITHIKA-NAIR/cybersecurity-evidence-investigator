Phishing checker: examine suspicious links and emails

A phishing message tries to make you trust an impersonated sender or take an unsafe action. EVIDENCE helps examine the content and the warning signs, with supporting evidence you can review.

What can be checked?

Paste a suspicious link or message, upload an original email, or inspect a supported attachment. The app can extract indicators, inspect public URL redirects and identify static file or message patterns. Optional licensed providers can add reputation data or an AI explanation when enabled.

How to interpret a result

A suspicious phrase is an indicator, not proof. HTTPS does not prove a site is legitimate. A file without detected indicators is not guaranteed safe. Missing evidence or unavailable providers may produce an Inconclusive result. Verify critical findings independently.

Already clicked?

If you shared a password, use the real provider's official app from a trusted device to change it, review sessions and enable multifactor authentication. If you sent money, contact the bank immediately through its official number. Open the app's recovery guidance for a situation-specific starting point.

Start a careful check

Remove passwords, tokens and personal information before submitting. Read the privacy notice. Never open a suspicious attachment just to collect more information.
