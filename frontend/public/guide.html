How to use EVIDENCE

EVIDENCE turns suspicious content into a structured investigation. Start with the type of evidence you have; phishing and other threats can appear in several categories.

1. Choose the evidence type

  • Links & websites: paste a complete public http:// or https:// address.
  • Emails: paste text or select the original .eml file. Original headers provide additional context.
  • Files & QR codes: select a supported non-empty file up to 10 MiB. The app inspects files without executing them.
  • Texts & messages: paste the relevant message and any suspicious URLs.

2. Sign in and submit

Choose Sign in, or Create an account and confirm your email. Remove secrets and unrelated personal information, read the processing notice, then submit. If you need to sign in after filling the form, you may need to re-enter your evidence. Do not submit again while the shield is loading. A slow service can take longer when free hosting wakes up.

3. Read the results in order

  1. Overview: read the assessment, confidence and limitations. Inconclusive means the evidence is insufficient or a required provider is unavailable.
  2. Supporting evidence: inspect observations and the evidence-backed sequence.
  3. Technical details: review relevant file, email, URL and reputation checks.
  4. What to do next: choose recovery steps for what actually happened.

Challenge Conclusion asks for another assessment of the same evidence; it is not an independent guarantee. Each investigation and review consumes a request allowance.

4. Revisit or delete

My investigations automatically loads previews of your latest 100 cases. Search checks those previews, case numbers, types and verdicts. Refresh reloads the list. Open a case to revisit it, or use Delete and confirm permanent removal.

When something goes wrong

Reconnect if offline. Sign in again if your session expired. Confirm your email if access is denied. If a limit is reached, wait for the stated retry period. If PostgreSQL is unavailable, new analysis is paused to protect persistence and quota controls. Contact __CONTACT__ without including secrets.

Already affected?

Open recovery guidance even without an account. For stolen credentials, use the provider's official app from a trusted device; secure your email first. If money is involved, contact your bank through its official channel. For a work device, contact your security team. EVIDENCE cannot perform recovery for you.
