Acceptable use policy

Use the website for defensive investigation

Submit material you own or have explicit permission to inspect. Preserve evidence responsibly and respect privacy, intellectual property and applicable law.

Prohibited activity

  • Unauthorized scanning, exploitation, credential theft, phishing campaigns or attempts to access another user's cases.
  • Uploading illegal material or confidential evidence without authority; sending passwords, private keys, access tokens or one-time codes.
  • Automated abuse, evading rate limits, overwhelming parsers or attempting to run payloads through the service.
  • Harassment, doxxing, false accusations or presenting automated findings as verified proof against a person.
  • Reselling external provider data or using integrations outside their licenses.

Report concerns

Contact __CONTACT__ with a description, time and relevant case identifier. Do not attach active malware or reveal secrets. The operator may restrict abusive activity and preserve information when lawfully required.
