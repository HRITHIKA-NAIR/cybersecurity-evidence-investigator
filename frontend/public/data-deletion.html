Delete your data

Delete an investigation yourself

  1. Open EVIDENCE and sign in to the account that owns the case.
  2. Choose My investigations. Your latest cases load automatically; use Refresh to reload them.
  3. Find the case, choose its Delete button and review the confirmation.
  4. Choose Delete permanently. The case, related metadata, findings and review results are deleted from active application storage.

This cannot be undone through the app. A failed request will show an error; check the list again before assuming deletion succeeded.

Delete your account or request all case data

Contact __CONTACT__ from the account's email address and request account deletion. Account deletion is currently a manual operator process. The operator verifies ownership, removes all linked cases and usage counters, revokes sessions and deletes the Supabase Auth account. A response must explain any justified retention or backup limitations. Do not send your password, tokens or one-time codes.

If you cannot sign in

Use Forgot password from the sign-in screen, then follow the recovery email on the same device and browser. If recovery fails, contact the operator using the account email address. Do not post private evidence in public repository issues.

Limits of deletion

Deleting active rows does not immediately purge hosting logs, historical backups or data previously processed by an external provider. The privacy notice explains the processing and current retention approach.
