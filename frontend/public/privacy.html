Privacy notice

Effective 23 September 2026. Operator: __OPERATOR__. Contact: __CONTACT__.

What we collect and why

Supabase Auth processes your email address, password authentication and session information to manage your account. The application stores your account identifier, submitted or extracted evidence, filenames and metadata, indicators, findings, review results and timestamps to provide your private investigation history. Upload bytes are processed temporarily; the application does not keep a separate raw-file archive. Extracted content may still contain personal information.

Submit only necessary evidence

Remove passwords, one-time codes, access tokens and unrelated personal details. Do not submit sensitive or confidential material, evidence about another person without authority, or material subject to workplace confidentiality restrictions. You can read recovery guidance without creating an account.

Services involved

  • Render hosts the website and API; Supabase hosts authentication and PostgreSQL. These providers may process operational logs and network identifiers under their own policies.
  • AI and reputation integrations are disabled by default. If the operator enables Google Gemini, extracted content and the evidence bundle can be sent to Google. Unpaid Gemini services may use submissions to improve products and involve human review; they are unsuitable for personal, sensitive or confidential evidence.
  • If VirusTotal is enabled, indicators such as domains, IP addresses and file hashes are looked up. This application does not upload raw files to VirusTotal. Provider retention is outside this application's deletion controls.
  • URL inspection may contact the public destination and redirect hosts, revealing a request from the server. Do not submit secret-bearing or one-use URLs.

Retention and deletion

Saved cases remain in active storage until you delete them or the operator removes them. There is no automatic case-expiry schedule in this version. Daily request counters support abuse limits and are not your investigation history. Hosting and provider logs and any backups follow separately configured retention; deleting a case does not immediately erase every provider-held copy. The operator must publish material retention or processor changes before they take effect.

Your controls and requests

Delete individual cases from My investigations. For account deletion, access, correction or other privacy requests, follow the data deletion page or contact __CONTACT__. We may need to verify ownership and consider applicable legal retention requirements. Never send passwords or identity documents unless a justified, secure process has been agreed.

Browser storage and recording

The app stores your authentication session and theme preference in your browser. It does not contain advertising trackers, analytics or session-replay recording. Anyone with access to your browser profile may access an active session. Use a private device and sign out when finished.

Audience, location and rights

This beta is intended for adults 18 and over. Provider processing may occur outside your country. Applicable rights, processing grounds, transfer safeguards and response deadlines depend on the operator and the places in which the service is offered. Contact the operator for details or to raise a concern. This notice must be reviewed against the actual deployment before public launch.
