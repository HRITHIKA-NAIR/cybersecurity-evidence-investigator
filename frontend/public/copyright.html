Copyright and removal requests

Third party software and icon license notices. The website uses installed system fonts and does not distribute a custom font.

Report a concern

Send copyright concerns to __CONTACT__. Include your name and contact details, the work you believe is infringed, enough information to identify the disputed material or page, the basis of your rights, and a clear explanation of the concern. Do not send private evidence unless requested through a secure channel.

What happens next

The operator will assess the request, verify relevant information and take action when appropriate. A private case identifier can help locate material without making it public.

DMCA applicability

This is a copyright reporting channel, not a claim that the operator has registered a US DMCA designated agent or qualifies for a safe harbor. A formal DMCA program, designated-agent registration and counter-notice process depend on the operator's activities and applicable law. The operator must obtain appropriate advice before claiming those protections.
